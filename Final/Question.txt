Prompt: "You are an expert academic research assistant and tutor. I have provided 4 research papers/sources. Read each source carefully and analyze them independently.
For EACH paper, create exactly 15 important questions with short answers (60 questions total).
The questions must focus on understanding the research paper from a research methodology, presentation, viva, and supervisor-discussion perspective.
Question Areas
Across the 15 questions for each paper, cover the following as relevant:
What is the actual problem addressed by the paper?
What research gap or limitation does it address?
What are the main objectives?
What is the purpose of the paper?
What dataset/data is used? Where does it come from?
Why is this dataset/data used?
What methodology or research method is used?
What model, algorithm, framework, technique, or procedure is used?
How is the proposed solution/approach achieved?
What type of analysis is performed: quantitative, qualitative, or mixed-methods?
What is evaluated?
How is the solution/method evaluated? What metrics, criteria, experiments, or baselines are used?
What are the main results?
What was achieved and what are the main outcomes?
What are the real-world impacts, applications, limitations, or recommendations?
Adapt the questions to the actual paper. Do not force a question if that topic does not apply. For example, if a paper does not use a conventional dataset, ask about the actual data, participants, case study, experiment, simulation, or evidence used.
Answer Requirements
For every question, provide a short answer of approximately 2–5 sentences.
Answers must:
Directly answer the question.
Be clear, concise, and easy to memorize.
Focus on the most important information.
Include important numbers, datasets, methods, models, metrics, and results when relevant.
Be based ONLY on the corresponding source.
Never mix information between papers.
Never invent or assume information.
If the source does not provide enough information, simply write:
"The source does not provide sufficient information to answer this."
Do not create explanations about missing information beyond this unless necessary.
Question Quality
Make the 15 questions specific to the paper, not generic.
Avoid:
Repetitive questions
Questions that only ask about the abstract
Trivial factual questions
Unnecessary background questions
Long multi-part questions
Prioritize questions that help me explain the paper to my supervisor, such as:
Problem → Research Gap → Objective → Purpose → Data → Method → Analysis → Proposed Solution → Evaluation → Results → Outcome → Impact
